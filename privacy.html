PlayPact Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-25

PlayPact helps families agree on how children earn game time through activities like reading and homework. This policy explains what happens to information in the app.

We don't collect your data

PlayPact has no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics. We don't collect, store, sell or share any personal information. The app only uses the internet if you turn on family sharing, and then only through your own iCloud account.

What stays on your device

To work, PlayPact keeps the following only on your device:

  • the activities and rules you set up, the focus sessions, and the history of minutes earned and spent;
  • the parent passcode and recovery code, stored as one-way encrypted fingerprints (hashes) in the device's secure Keychain;
  • settings such as free play days and the daily limit.

This information is included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) as usual for apps, and is removed when you delete PlayPact (the passcode is cleared the next time PlayPact is installed).

Family sharing (optional)

If you choose to share the child's PlayPact with a parent's phone, a summary (minutes saved, the activity in progress, play time, recent history and the activity rules) is stored in the child's iCloud account and shared only with the people you invite, using Apple's iCloud sharing. We can't see it. You can stop sharing at any time in PlayPact's Parent area.

Screen Time

If you turn on game blocking, PlayPact uses Apple's Screen Time features (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity). You choose the games in Apple's own picker; Apple gives PlayPact only anonymous tokens, so PlayPact never learns which apps or websites you picked or how they're used. This information doesn't leave your device.

Notifications

PlayPact can send a local notification when a focus session ends. It's scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to us.

Children

PlayPact is designed for families and is set up by a parent. Because we don't collect any information, we don't collect information from children.

Changes

If PlayPact ever adds features that send information off the device (for example syncing between a parent's and a child's device), we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details first.
