PlayPact

Build habits. Earn play.

PlayPact helps families agree on how children earn game time: reading, homework or practice earn minutes, and the games you choose stay locked until play time starts.

Getting started

  1. Open Parent and create a parent passcode. Save the recovery code it shows.
  2. Under Games to block, turn on Screen Time and choose the games.
  3. Adjust the activities and how many minutes each one earns.
  4. Optional: choose free play days (for example Wednesday and the weekend).

Common questions

How does the world leaderboard work? In the version that adds world rankings, a parent can enable Parent → World leaderboard and approve sharing with Apple Game Center. Finished activities count toward weekly and all-time focus-minute rankings, up to each activity's goal. Only the score and Game Center nickname are public; activity details and photos stay private. It is off by default and requires a Game Center account. Turning it off stops future uploads; past scores may remain with Apple. Weekly rankings reset Monday at 00:00 UTC. Devices using the same Game Center account share a rank, but their local totals are not added together.

I forgot the parent passcode. On the passcode screen, tap Forgot Passcode? and enter your recovery code. Without it, delete and reinstall PlayPact; this erases its history and balance.

The games didn't unlock. Earned minutes don't unlock games by themselves. Start play time in Parent, or let your child start it (Parent → Let my child start play time).

Can I add game time myself? In version 1.2 and later, open Parent → Add minutes on your child's device. Choose 1–180 minutes and an optional reason. The addition appears in History and in the shared family view. It adds saved minutes; start play time separately when your child is ready.

Can an activity count wins or finished projects instead of minutes? In version 1.4 and later, add or edit an activity in Parent and choose Times done under Rule, for things a timer can't measure, like winning a chess game or finishing a 3D model (there are chess and 3D model icons too). Your child taps I did it each time; nothing is earned until you approve it in Parent → Waiting for you to check on their device. When the approved times reach the goal, for example 3 wins, the minutes are added and the count starts again. The daily earning limits still apply. A parent's phone in family sharing shows what's waiting but can't approve it.

How long did my child actually use the chosen apps? In version 1.2 and later, open Parent → Games to block → App usage on the child's device for today's Screen Time report. It shows actual app and website use, rather than the minutes redeemed in PlayPact. Screen Time permission and a selection are needed, and Apple may take a little while to update the report. Usage isn't sent to your phone.

I can't delete apps or Home Screen bookmarks. Prevent deleting apps applies to the whole device, including bookmarks. In Parent → Games to block, tap Allow deleting for 15 minutes (version 1.2 and later); it turns back on by itself. Or turn it off there, then turn it on again.

The timer stopped and Pip wants a photo. In activities chosen in Parent → Pip's photo check (version 1.2 and later; homework to start with), Pip sometimes asks for a photo of the work. A photo within 5 minutes keeps all the time; later, the timer carries on from where Pip asked. If the camera can't be used, a parent can tap Let the timer carry on without a photo in Parent. Photos stay on the device for 7 days.

Sharing with my phone doesn't work. Both devices need to be signed in to iCloud. Send the invitation from the child's device (Parent → Family sharing) and open it on your phone with PlayPact installed.
